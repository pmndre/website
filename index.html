Lib. II The Divine Pymander

Poimandre, or The Vision

The Divine Pymander is one of the earliest of the Hermetic writings now extant. While probably not in its original form, having been remodeled and incorrectly translated since, this work undoubtedly contains many of the original concepts of the Hermetic cultus. The Divine Pymander consists of seventeen fragmentary writings gathered together and put forth as one work.

The second book of The Divine Pymander, called Poimandre, or The Vision, is believed to describe the method by which the divine wisdom was first revealed. It was after Hermes Mercurius Trismegistus had received this revelation that he began his ministry, teaching to all who would listen the secrets of the invisible as they had been unfolded to him.
